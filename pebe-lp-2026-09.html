Last chapter at Schwab — the engine matures, the platform changes

September's realized P&L of $1,194 is below the 8-month average. This requires honest context rather than explanation away. The month's activity was dominated by COIN roll management — three rolls across two bundle chains consumed $2,738 in close costs against $3,637 in re-entry premium. The roll cycles were net positive, but the friction reduced realized income in the settlement window. Booked premium of $5,541 — the third-highest of the operating period — confirms the engine was working productively. The gap between booked and realized is a timing artifact, not an underperformance signal, and $2,852 in open premium carries forward into October's settlement.

Two COIN bundles closed this month — both net positive wins under the bundle methodology. Bundle B (originating from the 182.50C on August 20) closed at +$519 after traversing 182.50→195→197.50 through three roll events. Bundle C (originating from the 200C on August 21) closed at +$1,074 through the same merged roll path. Bundle A (the 160→165→175→180 chain) remains open with a running net of +$162 — thin but positive after five rolls across 29 days. This bundle's 180C expires October 2 and will determine its final status.

CVNA scaled from 100 to 200 shares on September 28, funded by a $4,731 SGOV liquidation. The additional 100 shares were purchased at approximately $60 — well below the original August entry at $73, bringing the blended cost basis to $66.47. Two clean harvest cycles in September produced +$163 and +$191 at 82% capture each. CVNA is proving to be the stable, low-drama name the book needed — consistent premiums without the roll management overhead that COIN demands.

DAL's 81C ×2 position was defensively rolled to the 82C ×2 for a net credit of +$27. This is the standing below-cost-basis discipline applied correctly — DAL's cost basis sits at approximately $87 (blended after the Aug scaling), and the 81C strike was $6 below. The roll moved the strike closer to basis while generating a small credit. Three DAL contracts (82C ×2, 83C) expire October 2.
