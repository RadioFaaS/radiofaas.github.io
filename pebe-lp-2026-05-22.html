From the manager

The engine's best week on record

This week produced the strongest metrics of the operating period: an 81.5% win rate, 4.4-to-1 win-to-loss ratio, and a new single-trade record of $1,258 locked from a single COIN covered call — a contract written May 14 and closed May 21, capturing over 77% of maximum premium in 7 days. These numbers reflect a clean week of execution.

A note on methodology: this report now separates realized P&L (income locked from closed contracts) from open premium (income in flight from active positions). These are distinct. Realized income is confirmed — it is in the account. Open premium is the current market value of positions still running. We report them separately so that closing a profitable position always increases the realized number, making weekly progress unambiguous. This week, realized P&L increased by $3,258 — the largest weekly realization of the operating period — as six positions closed profitably.

A note on two contracts that closed earlier than their maximum capture potential. The standard target is 80%+ of premium captured before closing. Two COIN contracts closed at approximately 55% and 77% respectively. This was a discretionary judgment call, not a rule change: COIN had moved favorably after the write, and the decision was made to take the gain and free the capital. The engine does not have an early-close protocol. The 80% target stands. Human judgment that occasionally sees a clean exit is part of the design — it is the difference between this engine and a systematic product that cannot read the moment.

As of Friday close, the COIN covered-call book is fully unencumbered. All COIN calls have been closed. We hold the shares and write fresh contracts week of May 26. The current open book is light: two contracts (UAL, SOFI) with $627 in premium in flight.

Honest context: 81.5% win rate and 29.6% annualized ROC reflect favorable conditions — elevated implied volatility on COIN, clean exits, cooperative market environment. The long-run expectation remains 22–28% annualized. This report will reflect softer periods when they arrive, without adjustment.

— The manager

Methodology notes. Reporting scope is Feb 1, 2026 onward (post-tuning operating period). Jan 2026 is excluded from report scope; its realized P&L was −$768 and is noted for context only. All figures exclude positions outside the covered-call mandate and exclude long-term core holds. Realized P&L = net of each fully closed contract (STO proceeds minus BTC cost), counted once at close date. This is the primary income metric: it only increases when a position closes at a net gain. Open premium = STO proceeds received on currently active contracts, shown separately as income in flight. Average monthly realized = realized P&L from contracts that closed in Feb, Mar, Apr ÷ 3. Annualized ROC = average monthly × 12 ÷ $82,261 strategy capital. Win rate counts closed contracts only; assignments shown separately. Defensive rolls (R) are realized losses where a contract was repurchased and a new one written at a higher strike to maintain position. Benchmark comparisons (SPY, JEPQ) use total return vs. realized P&L plus open premium for PEBE — a framing that does not credit unrealized stock P&L on underlying positions. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
